# 🚀 Quick Start: Generate Retrospeck Character (5 Minutes)

## Option 1: Bing Image Creator (Easiest, FREE)

1. **Go to**: https://bing.com/create
2. **Sign in** with any Microsoft account (or create free one)
3. **Copy & paste this prompt**:

```
A friendly cartoon owl mascot character named Retrospeck, round chubby body, wearing oversized round amber-gold spectacles with thick frames, deep purple and indigo gradient feathers with warm amber accent feathers on wing tips, white chest feathers, large wise amber eyes, small yellow beak, holding a tiny notepad and pencil, modern flat illustration style, clean vector art aesthetic, professional tech branding, approachable and scholarly appearance, standing pose on a perch, white background, character design sheet showing front view, Pixar style rendering, soft lighting, 4K quality
```

4. **Click "Create"**
5. **Wait 30 seconds** - you'll get 4 variations
6. **Download** the one you like best!

---

## Option 2: Leonardo.ai (Better quality, FREE)

1. **Go to**: https://leonardo.ai
2. **Sign up** (free - 150 daily credits)
3. **Click** "Image Generation"
4. **Paste** the same prompt above
5. **Select model**: "Kino XL" or "Anime/Illustration"
6. **Click** "Generate" (uses ~10 credits)
7. **Download** your favorite!

## Option 3: ChatGPT (If you have Plus subscription)

## Next Steps After Generation

### 1. Save Your Character
Put it in: `assets/branding/character/`

### 2. Create App Icon
- Go to https://bing.com/create
- Use this prompt:
```
Simple cute owl face icon, large round amber-gold glasses, purple feathers, minimalist flat design, app icon, centered, white background
```
- Save as: `assets/branding/icons/icon-1024.png`

### 3. Resize Icons (Optional)
- Go to https://icon.kitchen
- Upload your 1024px icon
- Download full icon pack
