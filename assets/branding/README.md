## 🎨 Brand Guidelines

### Colors
- **Primary Purple**: `#8B5CF6` (Retrospeck's feathers)
- **Accent Amber**: `#F59E0B` (Glasses, highlights)
- **Deep Indigo**: `#6366F1` (Shadow/depth)
- **Background**: `#0A0A0B` (Dark mode)

### Character Traits
- **Wise but approachable**
- **Friendly, not corporate**
- **Scholarly with glasses**
- **Purple & amber color scheme**

### Usage Guidelines
- Minimum size: 24px (icon must remain recognizable)
- Always maintain glasses visibility
- Don't distort or change colors
- Maintain aspect ratio
